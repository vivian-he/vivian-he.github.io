DoorDashIn-Store Rewards

A new way to use DoorDash, driving +50% first-time conversion

Led discovery redesign and shaped future map-first exploration

  1. Customers knew DoorDash for delivery, not dining out

    A new rewards program let customers earn credits at restaurants, but challenged their existing mental model

  2. Explaining the product wasn't enough to get people to try it

    Education helped interested users, but didn't meaningfully improve first-time conversion

  3. Give people a reason to care before explaining how

    Lead with savings and familiar restaurants

  4. Make nearby rewards effortless to explore

    Streamlined map interactions based on browsing behavior

  5. From what's nearby to what's relevant to you

    Personalized collections and AI-powered recommendations

  6. What if all of DoorDash were map-first?

    Led a design sprint that influenced the product roadmap
